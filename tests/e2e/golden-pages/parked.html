Chicken Gyro Bowls

Ingredients

  • 1 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb chicken thighs
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 cups cooked rice

Instructions

  1. Whisk the Greek yogurt and garlic into a sauce. Refrigerate until ready to use.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet, add the chicken thighs and the garlic, and cook until browned, about 8 minutes.
  3. Assemble the bowls: spoon the rice into bowls and top with the chicken thighs and a dollop of the sauce.
