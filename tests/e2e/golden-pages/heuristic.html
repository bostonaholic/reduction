Cucumber Salad

Ingredients

  • 2 cucumbers
  • 1/4 cup rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Slice the cucumbers thinly.
  2. Whisk the rice vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, and salt together.
  3. Toss the dressing with the cucumbers and chill 20 minutes.
